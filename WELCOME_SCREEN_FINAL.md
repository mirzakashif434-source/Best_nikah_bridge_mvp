# Best Nikah Bridge — Final Welcome Screen

**Status: LOCKED / FINAL**

This is the approved opening/welcome-screen design for Best Nikah Bridge.

## Visual direction
- Soft warm cream/off-white background
- Deep Islamic green as the primary brand colour
- Gold accents and thin gold borders
- Clean, elegant, premium, calm Muslim matrimonial identity
- Subtle Islamic arch and distant mosque silhouettes
- No phone status-bar Wi-Fi or battery icons in the artwork
- Urdu language selector at the top-right

## Opening content order
1. Full Arabic Bismillah: `بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ`
2. `Welcome`
3. Heart + bridge mark
4. `BEST NIKAH BRIDGE`
5. Three trust/value cards:
   - Meaningful Matches / بامعنی رشتے
   - Verified Profiles / تصدیق شدہ پروفائلز
   - Private & Safe / محفوظ اور پرائیویٹ
6. Primary `GET STARTED` button with Urdu `آغاز کریں`
7. Secondary `SIGN IN` button with Urdu `لاگ اِن کریں`
8. Footer: `A trusted Muslim matrimonial platform` with Urdu supporting text

## Lock
Do not change the approved colour palette, overall composition, Bismillah, Welcome text, bridge/heart identity, or opening-screen content without the owner's explicit approval.
