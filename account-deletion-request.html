Delete your Best Nikah Bridge account

This public page lets Best Nikah Bridge users request deletion of their account and associated personal data even if they no longer have the app installed.

Request deletion by email

Tap the button below and send the request from the email address linked to your Best Nikah Bridge account. In the message, include the email address or user ID used for the account so we can identify it.

Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email app, email admin@bestnikahbredge.com with the subject Best Nikah Bridge Account Deletion Request.

In-app deletion

You can also request deletion inside Best Nikah Bridge from the account/profile area using Permanently Delete Account.

What is deleted

When the request is verified and processed, the Best Nikah Bridge account and personal data associated with that account are deleted from our active systems, including profile information and user-provided content linked to the account.

Data that may be retained

Limited information may be retained only when necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or enforcement of our terms. Any retained information is kept only for the applicable required period and is not used for ordinary account activity.

Important safety note

Do not send passwords, payment card details, identity-document images, or other unnecessary sensitive information by email. We may ask for limited information needed to verify that the request belongs to the account holder.
