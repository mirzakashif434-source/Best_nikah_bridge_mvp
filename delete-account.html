Delete Your Best Nikah Bridge Account

This page explains how users can request deletion of their Best Nikah Bridge account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the Best Nikah Bridge app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open your Profile or Account Settings.
  4. Choose the account deletion option, if available.
  5. Follow the instructions to submit your deletion request.

What will be deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, the user's account and associated personal profile information will be deleted, except information that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Data retention

Some information may be retained for a limited period when required by law or necessary for security and fraud-prevention purposes.

Contact

Users can contact Best Nikah Bridge support through the support/contact method provided in the app for account deletion assistance.
