# BEST NIKAH BRIDGE — NIKAH PROMISE PATH FINAL LOCK

**Status: FINAL / LOCKED — 2026-08-31**

## Production feature
After a mutual connection, Best Nikah Bridge may offer a private, consent-based **Nikah Promise Path** that helps two serious members progress respectfully toward family involvement and Nikah preparation.

### Stages
1. Intent to progress
2. Mutual preparation step
3. Family/Wali involvement (optional and consent-based)
4. Pre-Nikah discussion topics
5. Readiness confirmation
6. Nikah planning
7. Optional Nikah Completed milestone

## Mutual consent
- Each member chooses independently whether to participate in each step.
- No pressure, countdown, coercive language or forced commitment.
- A member's private choice not to proceed is not exposed as a rejection reason.
- A step unlocks only when the required parties have independently consented.

## AI role
AI may help prepare questions, explain the next step and summarize user-provided information. It must not make the marriage decision, guarantee compatibility, impersonate a scholar or issue religious rulings.

## Family Bridge
Where users choose family involvement, the app may provide a controlled invitation and only the information explicitly permitted by the member. Access can be revoked.

## Nikah milestone
The Nikah Completed milestone is voluntary and does not constitute legal or religious certification. Any memory/card generated from it is private by default and shareable only with explicit consent.

## Production rules
- Real authenticated users and production backend data only.
- Server-side authorization and state transitions.
- Auditable consent records.
- No fake/demo progress or fabricated family/Nikah status.
- Must integrate with existing privacy, block, report, moderation and mutual-chat controls.

## Final decision
The owner explicitly approved Nikah Promise Path for Best Nikah Bridge and requested it as a real release feature rather than a demo. This specification is locked. Changes require explicit owner approval.
