# BEST NIKAH BRIDGE — FAMILY BRIDGE FINAL LOCK

**Status: FINAL / LOCKED — 2026-08-31**

## Production feature
After mutual consent and where appropriate, members can create a private **Family Bridge** space to introduce selected family members/Wali and discuss marriage-relevant topics in a controlled environment.

### Supported areas
- Family introduction
- Family expectations
- Future country/city
- Career and living plans
- Children expectations
- Financial expectations
- Nikah preferences
- Possible timeline
- Family-to-family questions through a controlled Question Bridge

## Privacy and consent
- Family access is never automatic.
- The member explicitly chooses what information to share.
- Private one-to-one conversations remain private unless explicitly shared.
- Access can be revoked.
- Each family participant is authenticated and authorized for the specific Family Bridge.
- Questions/answers are stored and shared only according to explicit permissions.

## Safety
- Report, block and moderation controls remain available.
- No pressure to involve family.
- No sensitive/protected-trait inference.
- No fabricated family approval or Nikah status.
- Server-side authorization is mandatory.

## Production requirement
This is a **real production feature requirement**, not a demo/mock. It must use authenticated production data, secure server-side access controls and auditable consent. Missing backend/provider/security implementation is a release blocker and must not be replaced by fake data.

## Final decision
The owner explicitly approved Family Bridge / Question Bridge for Best Nikah Bridge and requested it for the release baseline. Changes require explicit owner approval.
