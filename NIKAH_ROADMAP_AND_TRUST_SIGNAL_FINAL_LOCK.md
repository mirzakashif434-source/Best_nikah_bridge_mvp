# BEST NIKAH BRIDGE — NIKAH ROADMAP & TRUST SIGNAL

**Status: FINAL / LOCKED — 2026-08-31**

## 1. Nikah Roadmap
For every mutual connection, provide a private, structured journey from serious interest toward Nikah:

1. Intent — confirm genuine marriage intention.
2. Compatibility — deen, character, family, children, finances, location, career, lifestyle and deal-breakers.
3. Family Introduction — optional consent-based Family/Wali involvement.
4. Important Questions — personalized pre-Nikah questions and mutual comparison.
5. Reality Check — surface strong similarities, important differences and topics that should be discussed before marriage.
6. Readiness — each person independently confirms readiness; never pressure either person.
7. Nikah Plan — target timing, family meeting, location/country and agreed next steps.
8. Nikah Completed — optional voluntary milestone for both members.

### AI behavior
AI may generate questions, summaries and preparation guidance. It must not make the marriage decision, guarantee compatibility, impersonate a scholar or issue religious rulings. Final decisions belong to the members and their families/Wali where appropriate.

## 2. Seriousness & Trust Signal
Create a private server-side trust/seriousness signal using legitimate safety and product signals such as:
- profile completeness and consistency;
- verified-account status;
- confirmed marriage intention;
- respectful usage patterns;
- report/block history and resolved moderation events;
- suspicious mass-like/message behavior;
- financial/scam indicators;
- progression through appropriate family/roadmap steps where voluntarily provided.

### Rules
- Never expose a hidden score in a misleading way or claim that a person is "good" or guaranteed safe.
- Do not infer sensitive traits or make religious, racial, health, political or other protected/sensitive judgments.
- Do not use the signal as an automatic marriage recommendation.
- Give users understandable safety explanations where a meaningful restriction occurs.
- Keep safety/moderation decisions server-side and auditable.
- Allow authorized admins to review serious risk cases.

## 3. Product positioning
These features are intended to make Best Nikah Bridge a **safe journey from matching to serious Nikah preparation**, rather than a dating/chat-first product.

## Final decision
The owner explicitly approved both **Nikah Roadmap** and **Seriousness & Trust Signal** as final product features. This specification is locked and must not be changed without explicit owner approval.
