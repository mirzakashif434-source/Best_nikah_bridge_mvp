Use the subject Best Nikah Bridge - Account Deletion Request and include the account email address so the request can be matched to the correct account. Do not send passwords, OTPs, identity documents, payment-card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information by email.

In-app deletion

Signed-in users can also open Privacy Control Center → Delete My Account & Data inside Best Nikah Bridge and complete the authenticated deletion flow.

What deletion means

After an authenticated deletion request is completed, Best Nikah Bridge deletes the account data covered by its deletion process. Information that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction, or dispute-resolution reasons may be retained only as described in the privacy policy and applicable law.
