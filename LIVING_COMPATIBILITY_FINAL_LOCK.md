# BEST NIKAH BRIDGE — LIVING COMPATIBILITY FINAL LOCK

**Status: FINAL / LOCKED — 2026-08-31**

## Purpose
Keep Best Nikah Bridge useful as members' real marriage circumstances and preferences evolve, without turning the app into a surveillance system or making hidden sensitive inferences.

## Production features
1. **Preference Evolution** — members can update country, location, timeline, family involvement and other matching preferences. The app can politely ask whether matching preferences should be refreshed after a meaningful user-initiated change.
2. **Compatibility Timeline** — show how compatibility is currently supported across agreed areas rather than presenting a single permanent percentage as a guarantee.
3. **Mutual Connection Health Check** — after a mutual connection, each person can independently review communication comfort, family progress, unresolved differences and whether the Nikah timeline is still aligned.
4. **Change Alerts** — where a member chooses to share an important matching-preference change, notify the mutual connection and let them review the change.
5. **Adaptive AI Questions** — AI can generate fresh pre-Nikah questions based on the current roadmap stage and user-provided context, without making the marriage decision.
6. **Modular Architecture** — matching, safety, wallet, AI, payments, verification and roadmap services remain modular so providers/models can evolve without rebuilding the entire app.

## Privacy and safety rules
- Never silently infer sensitive/protected traits from behavior or conversation.
- Never claim compatibility, trustworthiness or marriage success is guaranteed.
- User-controlled preferences remain the source of truth.
- Health checks are private by default unless a user deliberately shares the result.
- Change alerts reveal only the preference change the member elected to share.
- AI guidance is supportive and does not issue religious rulings or replace family/Wali or qualified scholarly advice.
- Safety and moderation signals remain server-side and auditable.

## Release requirement
This is a **real production feature specification**, not a demo/mock. Any implemented component must use authenticated production data and server-side controls. If a provider or backend capability is not yet configured, it is a release blocker rather than a fake implementation.

## Final decision
The owner explicitly approved Living Compatibility as a permanent Best Nikah Bridge feature and requested it for the release baseline. Changes require explicit owner approval.
