Best Nikah Bridge — Child Safety Standards

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Zero tolerance: Best Nikah Bridge prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation, sexualization of minors, sextortion, trafficking of children, and any other conduct that endangers children.

1. Scope

Best Nikah Bridge is a serious Muslim matrimonial service intended for adults seeking marriage. These standards apply to users, profiles, messages, media, reports, and other content or behavior associated with the service.

2. Prohibited conduct and content

Users must not create, upload, request, share, distribute, promote, or facilitate CSAM or any sexual exploitation or abuse of a child. Grooming, sexual communication with minors, sextortion, trafficking, and attempts to arrange sexual contact with a child are prohibited.

3. Reporting child-safety concerns

Users can report suspicious or abusive behavior using the reporting tools available in Best Nikah Bridge. Reports may include child-safety concerns, threats, harassment, financial scams, or other serious violations. Users should provide enough information for the safety team to identify and review the concern.

For urgent danger or an immediate threat to a child, contact local emergency services or the appropriate law-enforcement authority in your country.

4. Action on CSAM and CSAE

When Best Nikah Bridge obtains actual knowledge of CSAM or CSAE, we will take appropriate action consistent with applicable law and these standards, which may include removing or restricting the material, disabling accounts, preserving relevant information where legally appropriate, and reporting confirmed illegal activity to the appropriate authorities.

5. Child-safety compliance

Best Nikah Bridge is committed to complying with applicable child-safety laws and regulations in the jurisdictions in which the service operates. Where applicable law requires reporting confirmed CSAM or child exploitation to a national or regional authority, we will follow the required reporting process.

7. Enforcement

Violations may result in content removal, account restriction, suspension, or permanent termination. Best Nikah Bridge does not permit users to use the service to endanger children or facilitate child sexual exploitation.

8. Updates

These standards may be updated when laws, platform requirements, or our safety procedures change. The current version will remain publicly accessible on this page.
